## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（10 句）
1. January came with frost on the lab windows.
2. David's thesis had a title that took two breaths to say: "A Study of Coastal Water Quality Using a Low-Cost Sensor Network." The department gave him a small facility by the harbor, where he helped install approximately twelve sensors.
3. The data arrived in a constant stream and filled a database that had outgrown its storage.
4. He documented every reading, and the project evolved as the numbers came in.
5. His supervisor, Professor Chen, an advocate of low-cost science, told him to stay alert and keep ample notes.
6. "The problem is not the equipment," she said.
7. "It is the interpretation, and the physics behind it." He spent his days trying to interpret the readings, to infer what had caused a dramatic decrease in one bay, and to determine whether the cause was the new factory or the warm winter.
8. The pattern was complex, with a dimension and a mass of small errors he had not expected.
9. A poll of the fishermen gave him a broad picture, and their observations matched his own.
10. The evidence was solid enough to justify a careful statement, hence the cautious wording of his summary.

### 第 2 段（11 句）
11. That week he sent his applications out.
12. The department forwarded a notice about a graduate conference, and David registered because two companies would be recruiting there.
13. He had a good background; what he lacked was confidence.
14. The first company sent a young female engineer who asked about his sensors.
15. "Your command of the practical side is impressive," she said.
16. "But can you work in a team?" He told her about the radio he and Mark had built at twelve, and she laughed.
17. The second interviewer was a former colleague of Professor Chen, a chief engineer in his fifties, slow of speech and hard to read.
18. He asked David to justify a number in his summary and to explain what an equivalent result would look like in a city of two million people.
19. "Very good," the man said at last.
20. "Not perfect, but very good, sir?" David said, and wished he had not.
21. Unfortunately, the hotel had lost his reservation, and the company could not accommodate him for a second night.

### 第 3 段（9 句）
22. On Saturday Mark dragged him to a baseball game on the north field.
23. Mark's department had won the campus championship the year before, but this year's team was weaker.
24. David, who had never held a bat, was put in the outfield, where he clapped at the wrong moments.
25. He watched the ball fly toward him, ran backward, tripped, and sat down hard.
26. He was not capable of catching anything that afternoon.
27. Mark jogged over.
28. "You are the most clumsy man I know," he said, "and I say that with affection." There was blood on David's knee, and Mark cleaned it with a tissue from his purse.
29. The team did not lose by much, but the champions walked off with their heads down.
30. "You performed badly," Mark said, "but you performed." "Anyhow," he added, "the beer is cold." They stopped to buy two bottles of a local beverage, and David did not expect to enjoy the afternoon, but he did.

### 第 4 段（9 句）
31. Sarah had changed her hair over the holiday, and the new haircut made her look serious.
32. She had also taken over a corner of the art studio, where she was experimenting with natural dye.
33. David found her there on Sunday, wearing old pants and a cotton shirt, her hands stained the color of tea.
34. Wool and cotton hung from a line like a strange rainbow.
35. "The walnut gives brown," she said, "and the beet gives a red that fades." David was fascinated.
36. "You are the most creative person I know," he said.
37. She shrugged and said that an actor in a film had started it; the costumes had been so vivid that she had looked up how they were made.
38. On Tuesday she asked David to tutor a first-year student in mathematics.
39. The boy was shy and slow with numbers, and David enjoyed it.

### 第 5 段（10 句）
40. Mark had joined a greenhouse project on the roof of the engineering building.
41. The idea was to grow vegetables with organic fertilizer and no chemical spray.
42. He showed David the beds: boxes of soil, a sack of compost, and a row of pots in which tomato seedlings were rising.
43. "We use milk against the fungus," he said, holding up a bottle.
44. "It sounds useless, but it works." David took a spade and helped him turn the soil.
45. They pushed a thin rod into each pot to support the plants.
46. The growing medium was a mix of soil and sand.
47. In the cold corner the soil would shrink away from the pots, and Mark would water it slowly.
48. "A greenhouse is a small country," he said.
49. "You have to preserve the peace." In the evening they put the milk in the refrigerator, and David made a dish of rice and eggs.

### 第 6 段（10 句）
50. The old laboratory had a ghost.
51. A graduate student in the 1980s had fallen asleep over an experiment, the tale said, and ever since the basement lights flickered at night.
52. Mark loved the story and repeated it to anyone who would listen.
53. David did not believe in ghosts, but he did believe in the threat of a deadline, and the basement was the quietest place to work.
54. One night he heard a chair move behind him; it was only a draft.
55. "Did the ghost frighten you?" Mark asked.
56. The gossip in the department was that Professor Chen had seen it too, but she had never confirmed the tale.
57. David decided that ignorance was sometimes a cure for fear.
58. He turned his chair to face the door, alert to every sound.
59. The reverse was also true: the more he worked, the less he thought about ghosts.

### 第 7 段（9 句）
60. In February the three of them took a train west and climbed a mountain.
61. The path began behind a village and rose through pine trees.
62. Sarah wore a waterproof jacket; Mark carried the water and complained.
63. The ridge overlooked the coast: the sea to the east, the mainland to the west, and the city between them.
64. A temple stood at the top, its red walls faded and its steps worn smooth.
65. An old woman told them, in a slow voice, that the temple was eight hundred years old.
66. On the way down a brief rain passed, and a rainbow stood in the air so vivid that Mark stopped in the path.
67. They watched a hawk fly below them until it was a dot.
68. David thought about the reason he had come to Haizhou, and it seemed simple.

### 第 8 段（10 句）
69. In March he took the train to the city and walked to the company's office in a glass tower.
70. The lobby was modern and quiet.
71. A young woman led him to the eighteenth floor, where the department occupied a long room with a layout he found hard to read.
72. The interview lasted an hour.
73. Then the chief engineer led him to the window.
74. "From this platform," he said, "you can see the whole harbor. In ten years half of it will be new." He spoke about the ladder a young engineer could climb if he was capable and patient.
75. David liked the man and disliked the building.
76. On the train home he read the contract twice.
77. Sarah's aunt, an attorney, had warned him about one clause, and he had asked the company to change it.
78. They had agreed.

### 第 9 段（12 句）
79. The decision was harder than the interview.
80. The company offered him a good salary in the north; a smaller firm in Haizhou offered less money and more freedom.
81. At dinner, Sarah split the bill three ways and listened while David listed the arguments.
82. "You are not choosing a job," she said.
83. "You are choosing a life." Mark, who had accepted a position with a railway company, said that the country's prosperity depended on people willing to go elsewhere.
84. "The nation is building everywhere," he said, "and the work is widespread. If nobody goes north, nothing gets built." David said he did not want to rely on a promise, and could not share an office with people he disliked.
85. Sarah said that was sensible.
86. "You should also think about housework," Mark said.
87. "Who will cook?" David laughed.
88. "You will." The smaller firm was convenient, a fifteen-minute walk from the campus.
89. "Anyhow," Mark said at the door, "you have a week. Do not let the fear decide for you." By morning David knew.
90. He would take the smaller firm and enforce one rule for himself: lest ambition make him forget why he had chosen engineering at all.

### 第 10 段（12 句）
91. The thesis defense was held on a bright morning in May, two days before the holiday.
92. David talked about sensors, salt, and the memory of the sea.
93. Professor Chen asked the hardest question, and he answered it badly, then better, then well.
94. "You have done solid work," she said.
95. "Now go and eat something." The three of them built a small fire on the beach that evening, against the rules, and sat until the flames went down.
96. "We survived," Mark said.
97. "Four years." Sarah looked at the water.
98. "What did we learn?" she asked.
99. David said that he had learned how to work, how to ask for help, and how to tell the difference between a problem and a fear.
100. It was not a complete education, he said, but it was a real one.
101. They allowed themselves one bottle of beer each and toasted the year.
102. When the flames died, they walked back in the dark, and the sea sounded behind them.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
