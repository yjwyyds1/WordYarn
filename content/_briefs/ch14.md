## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（9 句）
1. The first Wednesday of June marked David's arrival in Beijing.
2. The train reached the station at seven, and the heat hit him like a wall: thirty-five degrees centigrade, the forecast said, with more of the same all week.
3. The city lay at a low altitude on a flat plain, on the largest continent, and its summer climate was sticky and intense.
4. The size of the city astonished him.
5. His employer, a manufacturer of kitchen appliances, had rented rooms for its interns in a college dormitory near the third ring road.
6. A company car took him along a wide avenue lined with trees.
7. Everything seemed larger than Haizhou: the buildings, the roads, the crowds.
8. "You will get used to it," the driver said.
9. "Everyone does." David kept his bag on his knee and watched the city go by.

### 第 2 段（8 句）
10. The company occupied a tall building with a test kitchen.
11. David's mentor, Ms. Zhou, showed him the lab: a row of appliances, a wall of tools, and a shelf of files with an index for every model.
12. "Every test must be written in the daily report," she said.
13. "If it is not in the file, it did not happen." The office was flexible about hours, and the team was generally friendly.
14. There was a cat, the office pet, who slept on a pink cushion and ignored everyone.
15. On his first morning David attended a meeting where the manager made an announcement about the summer launch and the election of a new staff representative.
16. "The principal reason we are here," the manager said, "is the customer." David wrote it down.
17. At breakfast in the kitchen, a colleague in a skirt and a white shirt offered him a carrot.

### 第 3 段（12 句）
18. His task was to test a new air conditioner that could transmit its temperature readings to a phone.
19. The sensor was delicate, and the data had to be precise.
20. David set up the unit, cleaned the frame of the window, and covered the outer casing with a cloth.
21. The unit would undergo a series of tests.
22. The first results were a mess.
23. The machine would suck in air, heat it, and send a number that fell below the truth.
24. "Do not trust the display," Ms. Zhou said.
25. "Trust the thermometer." He measured the vapor, checked the inner and upper panels, and locked the lab at night.
26. The work demanded concentration, and the heat made it worse.
27. By the third day he was under stress and sleeping badly.
28. "No one is immune to this city," Ms. Zhou said.
29. "Drink more water."

### 第 4 段（10 句）
30. The other interns were friendly, and David soon found a partner in a boy named Xiao Chen.
31. He was seventeen, the youngest of his peers and still an adolescent in many ways: he laughed too loudly, played soccer in the corridor, and asked silly questions.
32. But he was clever and quick, and he knew the city.
33. "I grew up in a hut in the old quarter," he said.
34. "Not a real hut. A small house. My grandmother still lives there." He spoke about the pain of leaving his village, and about his desire to study science at college.
35. He carried a dictionary everywhere and loved abstract ideas.
36. Sometimes he was fearful about the future; sometimes he doubted himself.
37. "You will do well," David said.
38. Xiao Chen grinned, and the doubt seemed to leave him.
39. Their friendship was a support to both of them.

### 第 5 段（8 句）
40. On Sundays they explored the city.
41. They visited the zoo, where a camel stood in the shade and a tiger slept behind glass; they walked down a long avenue, past a cinema and a luxury shop; they listened to a street musician play the violin outside a bookshop.
42. Xiao Chen bought a small book of poetry and read a poem aloud, and David understood only half of it, but the sound was beautiful.
43. In the old quarter they found a narrow lane of low houses, some of them in decay, and a wall with a sword hanging above a door.
44. "It is for luck," Xiao Chen said.
45. A car horn sounded behind them, and they stepped off the road.
46. The streets were becoming familiar, and the sand from a building site blew into their faces.
47. The city was enormous, and every district had its own character.

### 第 6 段（11 句）
48. The launch was scheduled for the end of July, and in the second week of that month the team found a bug that posed a fundamental problem.
49. The phone app displayed the temperature in Fahrenheit, not centigrade, and the error could mislead a customer into setting the machine too high.
50. "This is a disaster," the manager said.
51. "We must delay the launch." The sales director opposed him; a delay would cost money, and he did not want to exclude the summer market.
52. The argument involved the entire team and lasted two hours.
53. "The customer is not a nuisance," Ms. Zhou said.
54. "A wrong number is worse than a late product." Everyone agreed with her.
55. The director had no conscience, the manager said; the delay was a burden, but the next ten days were crucial.
56. They agreed to extend the deadline.
57. Moreover, the fix was simple.
58. The phone would ring all evening with questions.

### 第 7 段（15 句）
59. They worked late every night that week.
60. David rubbed his eyes, drank bitter coffee, and stared at the screen until the numbers seemed to move.
61. A beam of sunlight crossed the lab at dawn.
62. On the third night he found it: the app converted the reading twice, once in the outer program and once in the inner one.
63. The mistake was small, but its effects were comparable to a large error.
64. He fixed the code, and the display corrected itself.
65. Eventually the readings held steady.
66. The speed of the fix surprised everyone.
67. It was the first time the team had worked together so closely.
68. The lab reacted with a shout of joy.
69. Xiao Chen banged the table with his hand, and the sound echoed in the corridor.
70. "You did it!" he said.
71. "We did it," David said.
72. Ms. Zhou brought breakfast and sat with them.
73. "This is the part of the job I love," she said.

### 第 8 段（9 句）
74. The launch went ahead on the new date, and the product was a success.
75. The company published a short report on its website and distributed a bonus to the team.
76. David received a grant for his return ticket and a letter of recommendation.
77. "You achieved more in eight weeks than some employees do in a year," Ms. Zhou said.
78. He did not quite believe her, but he was pleased.
79. At this stage the product was ready for the market.
80. The team took a photograph together: Xiao Chen in the middle, the manager on one side, and David at the back, trying to look serious.
81. The office cat walked across the keyboard and sat on the pink cushion, and everyone laughed.
82. For the first time David felt that he belonged in the city.

### 第 9 段（10 句）
83. The farewell dinner was held in a small restaurant near the office.
84. There was soup, fish, corn, and a plate of carrots; the waiter brought a cake with cream that someone had helped to whip.
85. The manager made a speech and gave each intern a small gift.
86. Xiao Chen, who had never drunk wine before, had two glasses and became happily drunk; he told everyone that David was his best friend and that he would visit Haizhou one day.
87. David laughed, but he felt a tear on his cheek when the evening ended.
88. There was sorrow in the goodbye, and something sweeter too.
89. He looked around the table at the faces he had come to know.
90. "I wish you could stay," Xiao Chen said.
91. "I will come back," David promised.
92. "That is a promise." They walked to the dormitory together, and the city seemed quieter than usual.

### 第 10 段（7 句）
93. The train home left on a Wednesday morning.
94. David slept for most of the journey, exhausted by the intense weeks, and woke only when the coast came into view.
95. The recovery took several days: he needed rest, took a day off, walked on the beach, and slowly the noise of the city left his head.
96. He thought about the meaning of the summer.
97. The internship had given him a relation to the working world that college could not: he had seen how a product was made, tested, and sold, and how a single mistake could affect thousands of people.
98. The guidance of Ms. Zhou had been the most valuable part.
99. He would go back to his studies in September, but he would not forget the experience, which had been hard and truly his.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
