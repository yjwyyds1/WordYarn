## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（9 句）
1. August came with a heat that made the campus feel like an oven, and on the ninth of August David, Sarah, and Mark signed up for the Green Volunteers.
2. It was the group's tenth anniversary, and the beach cleanup was its largest outdoor event of the year.
3. A bunch of students gathered outside the sports hall, where a truck waited with gloves, bags, and a manual of safety rules.
4. There was an excitement in the crowd.
5. The organizer, a graduate student with a strong voice, gave a short talk about precautions and started to delegate the tasks.
6. She gave each team a map with dots marking the worst spots.
7. "Whichever team you join, stay with it," she said.
8. "No one goes into the water alone. That is an absolute rule." The arrangement was simple, and the volunteers set to work with enthusiasm.
9. David looked at the sea beyond the stadium.

### 第 2 段（8 句）
10. The beach was crowded with holiday visitors, and the volunteers worked around them.
11. They collected bottles, nets, shoes, and an immense collection of plastic.
12. An elderly man in a straw hat worked beside David for an hour without a word, and became his companion for the morning.
13. "The sea gives us everything," he said at last.
14. "It deserves a little care." By noon David's skin was red, his neck burned, and he was a bit tired, though by then he was accustomed to the heat.
15. They washed their hands with soap and took a break in the shade of a fishing shed for lunch: bread, cheese, and a pinch of salt on tomatoes.
16. Beyond the bay, a fleet of small boats moved along the horizon, and the calm water was a mirror for a last ray of light.
17. Mark lay on the sand, unconscious of the time, and complained that his back hurt.

### 第 3 段（10 句）
18. In the afternoon they divided into teams and walked the shore with sample bottles.
19. Sarah showed David how to record the position of each sample with a small device.
20. "We take one every five hundred meters," she said.
21. "That is a small fraction of the coast, but it tells us where the pollution comes from." A sophisticated-looking instrument on a cord measured the salt and the temperature.
22. David wrote the numbers in a pad and updated the shared map on the website.
23. "If the readings are false, the whole report is worthless," Sarah said.
24. "So we do everything twice." The immense scale of the task did not worry her; she talked about the cycle of the tides.
25. They found a dead fish, and Sarah put on a glove before lifting it into a bag.
26. "We will test it for a gene that belongs to a harmful bacterium," she said.
27. "That will tell us whether the source is the village or the harbor."

### 第 4 段（7 句）
28. The village itself was a small settlement of white houses around a church, with a courtyard where an old horse carriage stood waiting for tourists.
29. It was nowhere near a city.
30. The volunteers received an invitation to supper from the village council, and a grandfather of one of the children told them the story of the place.
31. "This was a kingdom once," he said, "and a prince lived on the hill. My grandfather was a peasant, and he could not read a single word. Then the teachers came, and everything changed." He talked about the fight for independence and about the men who had tried to conquer the territory and failed.
32. He told stories that relate to the sea, and a young journalist recorded him on a small recorder.
33. "We have to protect it," the old man said.
34. "The sea is all we have."

### 第 5 段（10 句）
35. The next morning the volunteers visited the primary school.
36. The teacher welcomed them and explained that admission was free and that most of the children would be the first in their families to finish school.
37. David was given the oldest class.
38. He taught them a short poem, translated it line by line, and let them copy it in ink.
39. One boy dictated a line back with great seriousness, and David hid a smile.
40. The poem was a favorite with the children, particularly the youngest.
41. The classroom was tidy but small, and the children shared books in rows.
42. The school functioned with very little: no stadium, no swimming pool, and a playground with a single swing.
43. Yet the children were intelligent, curious, and easy to distract.
44. A girl asked whether David had a scholarship, and when he said yes, she said, "Then I will have one too." Her assurance stayed with him all day, and a seed had been planted.

### 第 6 段（11 句）
45. Trouble arrived at the end of the week.
46. A development company wanted to build a hotel on the headland, and its local client had already bought part of the beach.
47. The company's representative was aggressive.
48. He said the hotel would employ a hundred local people, that the volunteers' report was false, and that they were damaging the village's future.
49. "You are a bunch of students with a few bottles," he said.
50. "Nobody will believe you." Mark wanted to fight him, but Sarah held his arm.
51. "We do not need a fight," she said.
52. "We need evidence." The representative was aggressive but never violent.
53. The village council called a public hearing, and the volunteers were invited to present their findings.
54. David spent two days checking every number.
55. He knew that a single error could disappoint the people who trusted them, and that the first step was certainty.

### 第 7 段（10 句）
56. On the eve of the hearing, Sarah and David went back to the beach with a lamp.
57. They had noticed an odd smell near the rocks, and the map showed a gap in their samples.
58. They followed the smell along the shore and found a pipe hidden under a heap of stones.
59. It was concealed so well that nobody had seen it, but a thin cord of waste was flowing from it into the bay.
60. David photographed it and took a sample.
61. In the lab, a laser scanner and a small tester found traces of a drug used in the hotel kitchen.
62. The gene test confirmed it.
63. They waited for a replacement part for the tester.
64. "It is no longer a guess," Sarah said.
65. "This is proof." Signs were put up to prohibit swimming near the pipe, and the group used the evidence to locate the source and update the report.

### 第 8 段（13 句）
66. The hearing was held in the church hall, and the room was full.
67. The company's lawyer, his opponent, spoke first, with a smooth voice.
68. Then David stood up.
69. He spoke for ten minutes, quietly and clearly.
70. He showed the map, the photographs, and the gene result.
71. He explained that the pollution was small, but that it would grow; that the village depended on the sea; and that the company had a responsibility to protect it.
72. The company had tried to cheat the village, and at least some people knew it.
73. Everyone was free to express an opinion, and the room went silent.
74. There was a moment of silence, and then the applause began.
75. The council voted to forbid any building on the headland until a full study was complete and to seek an alternative site.
76. The article appeared the next morning, and the story was shared on the group's website.
77. "You were wonderful," Sarah said.
78. "You persuaded them." The design of the poster, the merit of the report, and the right of the village to clean water were all mentioned in the article.

### 第 9 段（10 句）
79. The last evening was a musical celebration of the group's anniversary.
80. A zero-waste supper was served in the courtyard: the village school band played, and the children sang a song about the sea.
81. A local waiter served a simple meal of fish, rice, and vegetables, and everyone was given a generous portion.
82. The volunteers were not paid a cent, but nobody minded.
83. "Tonight," the teacher said, "we thank you properly." She made a short speech about the value of volunteers and the wisdom of children.
84. "You came to teach," she said, "and you learned." The spectators clapped, and the children were happy.
85. Mark was given a small prize in a photo contest; he pretended to be surprised, but nobody believed him.
86. David looked around at the faces he had come to know and felt a bond with the people and the strength of the community.
87. The moral of the evening was simple: a small group could do a great deal.
88. The glow of the lamps and the calm sea made the night complete.

### 第 10 段（13 句）
89. Autumn came soon after that, and the volunteers returned to their studies.
90. David kept in touch with the teacher and with the boy who had promised to win a scholarship.
91. The village's case was successful: the hotel plan was dropped, and the council set aside money for a small reserve.
92. The group published a supplement to the report, and the publication was read far beyond the village.
93. David did not think of himself as a hero.
94. He had done a small part, and the work would continue without him; the village owed him no debt.
95. The summer had changed his perspective.
96. He had seen that a community could be strong when it acted together, and that independence was not the same as isolation.
97. On his last evening he hiked along the coast with Sarah and Mark, and watched the sun set over the horizon.
98. It had been a worthwhile summer.
99. "Same time next year?" Mark said.
100. "Same time," David said.
101. It was a quiet promise, and enough.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
