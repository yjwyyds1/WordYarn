## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（9 句）
1. The last Saturday of August fell at the end of the summer vacation, and Sarah proposed a day of sightseeing in the old town.
2. David had never been downtown, so he agreed at once.
3. They met at the campus gate at nine, bought tickets, and climbed aboard the bus.
4. The traffic was heavy, and the bus moved slowly along streets crowded with tourists who seemed to invade the city every weekend.
5. The city had spread into the countryside, and the bus passed new apartment blocks before the streets narrowed.
6. Mark killed time by naming the shops they passed: a bakery, a shoe shop, a mall with an enormous glass roof.
7. Near the river the old buildings leaned toward each other as if they were talking.
8. David, a stranger to downtown, followed Sarah.
9. "When we get there," she said, "you will see the real city." He looked out of the open window and said nothing.

### 第 2 段（13 句）
10. Breakfast was the first business of the day.
11. The bakery on the corner was small but excellent, and the smell of bread reached them from the doorway.
12. The baker, a round woman with flour on her hands, sold them a warm loaf, a jar of honey, and two bars of chocolate.
13. "Everything here is fresh," she said.
14. "That is the only ingredient that matters." They ate at a cafeteria across the street, where the ceiling was low and the tables were crowded.
15. Mark ordered steak with rice and a bowl of peas; David chose soup and a mug of tea.
16. "You should drink it while it is hot," the waiter said.
17. The food was substantial and the quantity generous.
18. The gross weight of the loaf was nearly a kilo.
19. David watched the bubbles in his tea and decided the meal was worth every yuan.
20. The charge was small, and the consumer beside them, an old man with a newspaper, agreed.
21. "Plenty of food, plenty of talk," he said.
22. "That is a good morning." The difference between this cafe and the campus canteen was noticeable.

### 第 3 段（10 句）
23. After breakfast they walked down to the harbor.
24. The wind was stronger there, and the water slapped against the stone.
25. They went aboard an old fishing boat that now carried sightseeing groups; the owner showed them a coil of rope and explained that each fiber had to be checked before the boat went out.
26. "A rope is only as strong as its weakest fiber," he said.
27. David looked over the side at the view of the harbor and saw a crab moving sideways on the bottom, its claw raised like a small hand.
28. A shell lay on the pier.
29. Gulls and pigeons fought over a piece of bread, and a fisherman baited a hook with a worm.
30. The landscape was rough and beautiful: gray water, black rocks, and distant islands.
31. For a moment David glimpsed the city as it must have looked a century ago.
32. An occasional bat flew out from under the roof of a shed, though it was still day.

### 第 4 段（10 句）
33. At noon they went to a small church for the wedding of Sarah's cousin.
34. The building was old and cool inside, with a painted ceiling and a heavy curtain behind the altar.
35. The minister, a kind man with a quiet humor, welcomed the guests and asked them to sign the register.
36. Eighteen guests came, including David and Mark, invited at the last moment.
37. The bride wore a white dress and carried a single rose; the groom looked as if he might faint.
38. The minister spoke about patience and apology, and about how marriage binds two families.
39. "A spouse is not a servant," he said, "but a partner." The bride's mother began to cry and pressed a handkerchief to her eyes.
40. Sarah herself wiped her eyes and pretended she was not crying.
41. After the ceremony the reception moved to a garden behind the church, where a saint's statue stood among the flowers.
42. The previous week had been rainy; this afternoon was perfect.

### 第 5 段（11 句）
43. On the way to the market they passed a jewelry shop, then a narrow lane of clothing stalls.
44. Sarah stopped to look at a scarf, and David bought a small silver ring for his mother.
45. The shopkeeper explained the difference between real gold and false gold, and let them examine the jewelry.
46. The scarf was soft and smooth, and its size was right, but the price was not.
47. "I can lower it," the woman said, "provided you buy two." Sarah bought one and David another; the woman removed the tags and wrapped them.
48. The garment was trimmed with a thin line of silver thread, and its image in the mirror pleased Sarah.
49. David noticed a leak in the ceiling and a line of dirt on the wall.
50. "The owner should fix that," he said.
51. The woman shrugged.
52. "The owner lives in another city," she said.
53. "Ownership is easy from far away." A license hung framed on the wall beside an old photograph.

### 第 6 段（8 句）
54. At the end of the lane stood a small office where an old typist worked at a desk.
55. A sign said she had been established there for forty years and could type letters for anyone.
56. "It is a dying profession," she said, "but I am too old to specialize in anything else." A retired headmaster was waiting for a letter to his former students, and a young volunteer from the university was assisting him with it.
57. The headmaster liked to boast that he had taught three ministers and one deputy.
58. A committee had put up a notice about the popularity of the old market, and a powerful businessman had promised money for the roofs.
59. David asked the typist how she earned a living now.
60. "Carefully," she said.
61. "And I take care of the words." Her explanation was simple, and David liked her for it.

### 第 7 段（13 句）
62. In the afternoon a small dispute broke out in the market.
63. A tourist claimed that he had been charged twice for a bowl of noodles, and the shopkeeper denied it.
64. A crowd gathered.
65. The deputy from the local office arrived and asked both men to explain.
66. The tourist's defense was weak; he had no receipt.
67. The shopkeeper's account was clear, and she had a witness.
68. The deputy listened, asked a few questions, and resolved the matter in ten minutes.
69. The tourist apologized, and the shopkeeper accepted his apology with good tolerance.
70. "It is a misunderstanding," she said.
71. "It happens." There was a faint smell of smoke in the air, and for a moment the street was deadly quiet.
72. David thought about how quickly a small thing could become serious.
73. "You ought to write about this," Sarah said.
74. "It is a good story." He made a mental note and wrote two lines in his notebook.

### 第 8 段（9 句）
75. Before dinner they visited a small science exhibition in the old library.
76. A student from the university demonstrated an excellent experiment: he connected a coil of wire to a battery, and the coil began to emit a low hum.
77. "We compress the air here," he said, pointing to a diagram on the wall, "and this thermometer measures the change." A monitor beside the table showed the temperature in red numbers.
78. The explanation was clear, and the instructions were printed in large letters.
79. David, who had always liked machines, asked how the coil was made.
80. The student showed him a cross-section of the wire.
81. The crowd behaved well, and several children asked questions.
82. "The whole thing is simpler than it looks," the student said.
83. David wrote down the main points and thought that the afternoon had been more fruitful than any lecture.

### 第 9 段（11 句）
84. They rested in a park near the harbor, where the grass was cool and the wind carried the smell of salt.
85. Two boys were playing tennis on a cracked court, and a third was shooting arrows at a target tied to a tree.
86. One boy had a bandage on his thumb and sweat on his face.
87. An old gardener was trimming the hedges with slow, steady movements, and the sunset began to dominate the sky.
88. The light turned from gold to rose to a deep, gradual blue.
89. A critic might have called the park small, but David did not care; it was rarely so full.
90. It was a nice evening.
91. David lay back on the grass and looked at the clouds.
92. "This is the best part of the day," he said.
93. "The city is strange and noisy, but this is peaceful." Sarah nodded.
94. "You are becoming a philosopher," she said.

### 第 10 段（12 句）
95. Afterwards they took the bus home through the evening traffic.
96. David's account of the day filled three pages of his notebook, and he added a few additional details on the back: the taste of the honey, the color of the bride's rose, the sound of the coil.
97. He thought about the people they had met and the strange mixture of wealth and need in the old town.
98. He resolved to return sometime before the end of the term.
99. "You ought to come with us next time," he told Mark, who had fallen asleep against the window.
100. David did not wake him.
101. He was mature enough to know that some days could not be explained, only remembered.
102. The future was uncertain, and problems would arise, but he would persist.
103. He did not believe in fate, but he believed in attention.
104. He had made a secret promise to himself: he would not let the city confuse him or frighten him.
105. He would learn its language, and the learning would be fruitful.
106. The bus turned forward onto the main road, and the lights of the harbor faded behind them.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
