## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（6 句）
1. The first Monday began with a queue that stretched from the registration hall to the garden gate, and David joined it with his bag on his shoulder and his notebook in his hand.
2. The line moved slowly, but nobody seemed to mind; it was a good place to meet a classmate.
3. Ahead of him stood a tall girl in jeans and a brown coat, resting her chin on her hand, who turned around, smiled, and asked whether he was also waiting to confirm his student status.
4. Her name was Sarah, and she spoke with an easy grace that made the queue seem shorter.
5. A notice on the wall, printed in a type that was hard to read, gave the directions regarding the registration desk.
6. David examined it twice, cautious as always, and then followed Sarah through the crowd.

### 第 2 段（6 句）
7. The registration itself was simple.
8. The clerk confirmed his name, checked his photograph against the original form, and handed him a card that would be his permanent pass to the library.
9. "Keep it safe," she said.
10. "It is your qualification for everything on this campus." Owing to the number of new students, the office had moved the whole process into the big gym, where six desks had been set up in a straight line and a speaker on a stand repeated the same instructions every few minutes.
11. The speaker's voice was calm, and it conveyed more patience than the printed notices ever could.
12. By noon David had a card, a timetable, and a small map of the campus folded into his pocket.

### 第 3 段（7 句）
13. In the afternoon the new students met their academic advisor, a leading professor at the institute of engineering.
14. She sat behind a cabinet of files and asked each of them, one by one, what subject they wanted to study.
15. David said he was not sure, and she did not seem surprised.
16. "The majority of first-years are not sure," she said.
17. "That is why you are here." She explained the sequence of courses, the basis on which the classes were ranked, and the liberty every student had to change direction after the first year.
18. "Do not be afraid of beginning again," she told them.
19. "A degree is not a straight road. It is a triangle: three sides, and every one of them leads to the same place." David wrote the whole speech in his notebook, consulted the professor about his own choices afterwards, and understood only part of it.

### 第 4 段（5 句）
20. Later that week the library opened its doors to the new students, and David spent a lovely afternoon inside.
21. The reading area was quiet, and daylight poured through the high windows onto the desks, where a hundred lamps waited for the evening.
22. The librarian showed them how to search the online catalogue, how to use the keyboard without waking the room, and how to fetch a book from the closed shelves.
23. "Whatever you need," she said, "we can find it. But you must learn to utilize the catalogue first." David found an original edition of an old engineering text, its brown cover faded by time, and carried it to a seat by the window.
24. For a moment the noise of the campus faded, and he was alone with the book, the sunlight, and the quiet.

### 第 5 段（8 句）
25. The club fair filled the sports field on Saturday.
26. Every sport had a table, and every table had a display of photographs: the volleyball club, the running club, the archery club with its bows, the movie club with its posters of old films.
27. A student commission had organized the whole event, and the membership fees were small.
28. Sarah, in a blue dress, was running the book stall.
29. There was even a charity auction of old books, and David bought a novel for almost nothing.
30. Mark signed up for the sheep and cattle club — a joke, since his family kept sheep — and David, after some hesitation, put his name down for volleyball and for the English workshop, where students met once a week to enhance their spoken English.
31. A guest speaker from the city's leading newspaper gave a short talk about the trade of writing, and David listened with more interest than he expected.
32. The club president, a cheerful senior with the university's eagle on his badge, told them that the club was an asset to the campus, and nobody laughed, because it was true.

### 第 6 段（10 句）
33. On the northern side of the field, a campus officer gave a short talk about crime and safety.
34. "The campus is safe," he said, "but safety is a habit, not a gift." It was obvious that most students had come for the free tea rather than the lecture, but David listened.
35. He had never given much thought to such things before, and the talk made him see the place differently.
36. Sport, he was discovering, was also a different kind of lesson.
37. He was weak at volleyball and worse at running, and the first training session left him with a headache and a stubbed toe.
38. But fortunately, the coach was patient, and the team's defeat in the first practice match did not depress anyone for long.
39. "Effort counts more than ability," the coach said, and David, clenching his fist, decided to believe him.
40. By the end of the month he could serve the ball over the net, and the captain — the hero of the team — actually smiled at him.
41. The other team did not give an inch in the return match, and the crowd loved every minute of it; it was human nature to want to win.
42. The reward was small, a bottle of water and a nod, but it felt permanent.

### 第 7 段（10 句）
43. One Saturday evening at the end of September, a parcel arrived for David, tied with string and fastened with tape.
44. Inside were a jar of his mother's jam, a pair of warm socks, and a photograph of his grandparents.
45. His ancestors had been fishermen for four generations, and the photograph showed them on the beach, poor but proud, with the sea behind them.
46. David remembered the poverty of his childhood without bitterness; it had taught him to count every coin.
47. His monthly allowance was small, but he had learned to live within it.
48. He wrote a long letter home that night, and he did not mention the headache or the loneliness, because some things could not be conveyed on paper.
49. He wrote about the library, the clubs, and the kind professor, and he asked about the cat's injured paw and his grandmother's illness.
50. Then he finished his English homework, a short composition about the first week, and found that the words came easily.
51. His mother's reply, which arrived a week later, was full of small news: the apple pie had won a prize at the village fair, and the neighbors had asked about him.
52. He was glad, and more proud than he could say.

### 第 8 段（7 句）
53. The science workshop was the surprise of the term.
54. The professor, an influential man with a quick smile, began the first experiment by asking the class to measure a volt with an old receiver and a tank of water.
55. "Science is not a collection of facts," he said.
56. "It is a way of asking." They worked in pairs, and David's partner was a quiet boy from the north, who turned out to be very good with a hammer and a screwdriver.
57. The two of them devised a small device for measuring current, and although it was not pretty, it worked.
58. The professor examined it, nodded, and said that the best ideas often emerged from the simplest materials.
59. The whole class was pleased, and for the first time David felt that the domain of engineering might really belong to him.

### 第 9 段（6 句）
60. By the end of the month the campus had settled into a rhythm.
61. In accordance with the timetable, the mornings began with mathematics, and the afternoons were given to experiments and workshops.
62. The posters on the notice boards had begun to fade, the first frost had touched the garden, and the wind would blow cold through the corridors.
63. David had adapted to the routine, though he still tended to get lost in the laboratory building, and he had made a few friends apart from Mark and Sarah.
64. The daily grind of classes and sport was softened by the weekends, and the science workshop was the best part of it all, especially the long experiments that ran past dusk.
65. The football team united the dormitory in a way that nothing else could, and on match days even the poor students who could not afford tickets watched the live broadcast in the common room.

### 第 10 段（7 句）
66. The club president resigned in October, and Mark, to everyone's surprise, was elected in his place; it was the product of his loud voice and his endless energy.
67. Naturally, he became the victim of a dozen practical jokes, and he took every one of them with a grin.
68. There was scarce room for complaint, and whatever small troubles came, they passed quickly.
69. His will to sustain the effort never faded, and somehow the room had become his home.
70. On Sunday David called home, and his mother's voice seemed to cross the miles and sit beside him.
71. Yesterday he had been a stranger here.
72. Today the desk was his, the notebook on it was his, and the chapter had only just begun.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
