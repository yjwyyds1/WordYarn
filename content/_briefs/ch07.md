## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（12 句）
1. December arrived with a cold wind, and the final examinations were four weeks away.
2. The professor of mathematics gave the class a talk about preparation.
3. "You cannot accumulate knowledge in the last week," he said.
4. "Make a definite plan, get adequate sleep, and work against the clock every day." David wrote the advice on a card and pinned it above his desk.
5. His notes had accumulated all term, but they were a mess, and he carried a load of books to the common room every night.
6. It was a great deal of work, but there was no other way.
7. That evening he and Sarah met to build a strategy.
8. They divided the month into four sections, one for each subject, and agreed to meet at seven.
9. Behind the library, a small study room was usually free.
10. The plan was simple, and it would ensure that no hour was wasted.
11. "No excuses," Sarah said.
12. "And no coffee after ten." David laughed, but he wrote it down.

### 第 2 段（9 句）
13. The study group grew to four: David, Sarah, Mark, and a quiet graduate student named Li, who served as their unofficial assistant.
14. Li was patient and specific.
15. "Do not try to learn everything," she said.
16. "Learn the part that the exam will test." They coordinated their notes and collaborated on a single summary.
17. Sarah read it aloud and then took biology, because she loved the subject; she drew a sketch of the human body on a large sheet of paper.
18. Mark took the language section, though he hated nouns and could not tell a verb from a stone.
19. David took mathematics, where he could multiply and subtract faster than anyone.
20. "A couple of hours a day is enough," Li said, "if the hours are regular." Sarah played an opera on the radio, softly, while they worked.
21. They spread their books across two tables and stayed until the library closed.

### 第 3 段（11 句）
22. The library became a second home again.
23. The reading room was full by eight, and students occupied every seat.
24. David found a place on the balcony, where the air was cooler, and put a thin cushion on the chair.
25. The assistant librarian showed him how to find old exam papers.
26. "They are not a secret," he said.
27. "They are a resource." The author of their mathematics textbook had written a short guide to problem solving, and the library kept a provision of copies behind the desk.
28. The guide's appeal was simple: ask the question first.
29. David borrowed a copy and read it in an hour.
30. The library's service was excellent, and the old building had a quiet charm.
31. "The first step," the author wrote, "is to define the question." It was a simple idea, but it changed the way David studied.
32. He began each session by writing the question at the top of the page.

### 第 4 段（10 句）
33. By the second week the fatigue showed.
34. Mark fell asleep over his notes, and Sarah grew short with everyone.
35. One evening an argument broke out over a borrowed pen, and an insult was exchanged that nobody meant.
36. Mark choked on a biscuit and had to drink a glass of water; later he took a pill for his headache.
37. Anger filled the room, and for an hour nobody spoke.
38. David suggested a compromise: they would stop at nine, walk around the field, and start again in the morning.
39. "We are all emotional," he said.
40. "It is the exam, not us." Sarah apologized, and Mark made a foolish joke that broke the mood.
41. By morning the quarrel had healed, and they finished the week in a quieter spirit.
42. The pressure did not disappear, but they learned to manage it.

### 第 5 段（15 句）
43. The first exam was on a Thursday morning, in the great hall.
44. The professor walked between the rows and gave the signal to begin.
45. "You may open your papers," he said.
46. A student with a disability had been given extra time, and nobody complained; it was a common rule, and a fair one.
47. For a moment the only sound was the rustle of pages.
48. David read the first question twice, took a breath, and began to write.
49. The exam was not a weapon, he told himself; it was a conversation with the subject.
50. He worked through the questions in order, careful and steady.
51. Everyone was nervous, without exception.
52. Outside the tall windows, snow was falling.
53. When the bell rang, he had answered every question except one.
54. Sarah was waiting on the porch.
55. "How was it?" she asked.
56. "Fair," he said.
57. "Not easy, but fair."

### 第 6 段（12 句）
58. After the last paper they went to a small restaurant to celebrate.
59. The menu was simple: soup, salad, fried tomatoes, and cake.
60. Steam rose from the soup, and the waiter lifted the lid from a pot and let them taste the sauce.
61. The flame under the pot burned blue.
62. One tomato in the salad was rotten, and Mark made a face.
63. "Send it back," he said.
64. "No," Sarah said.
65. "It is one bad tomato, not a tragedy." They ordered a bottle of wine, though they were under age, and the waiter pretended not to notice.
66. "Hi," said a voice behind them; it was Li, who had come with two friends.
67. They pushed the tables together, and the celebration grew.
68. "Super," Mark said, raising his glass.
69. "We survived."

### 第 7 段（11 句）
70. On Saturday they took a break and went to the hills.
71. They jogged the first kilometer to warm up, then walked along a trail through the fields.
72. The air was cold and clean, and the oxygen made David feel awake.
73. They saw a snake on the path, a thin green line in the grass, and Mark leaped back with a shout and seized David's arm.
74. "It is more afraid of you," Li said.
75. They did not explore far; an ugly grey building stood at the top of the hill, and a tent was pitched beside a stream.
76. They ate lunch on a rock and looked at the scene below: farms, roads, a river, and the globe of the sun sinking behind the hills.
77. The smell of wet earth rose from the grass.
78. Darkness began to descend.
79. David bathed his face in the stream and felt the fatigue lift.
80. It was a peaceful afternoon.

### 第 8 段（12 句）
81. On Sunday David called home.
82. His father, who drives a truck, complained about the price of gasoline.
83. The family's income had risen, and he had inherited a small estate from an uncle.
84. "The rent from the field is not much," he said, "but it is regular." He asked about David's exams, and David told him about the study group and the library balcony.
85. "I am proud of you," his father said, and David felt a surge of pride.
86. His grandmother took the phone and spoke in the local dialect; he understood every word.
87. After the call, David wrote a letter and enclosed a small donation for the village school.
88. The school sent a handsome thank-you note.
89. He also sent a photograph of the group at the restaurant.
90. "These are my friends," he wrote.
91. "They are loyal, and their loyalty matters more than their marks." He promised to keep in contact.
92. Outside, a bus brake squealed.

### 第 9 段（13 句）
93. Results came two weeks later.
94. David stood in the corridor and read the list on the wall.
95. Nearly a hundred students stood there, and a quarter of them were smiling.
96. He had passed every subject, with eighty in mathematics and only a minor problem in biology.
97. The pass rate was high.
98. The recognition from his professor was a single sentence: "Well done; your work is fairly consistent." It was not much, but he felt a deep pride.
99. Mark came to congratulate him, and the two of them laughed at their own relief.
100. The only criticism came from Mark, who said the food in the hall was worse than the exam.
101. Unlike Mark, who wanted a party, David wanted only to sit quietly and think.
102. From this distance, the term looked shorter than it had felt, and there was a shift in his thinking.
103. His imagination had done half the work.
104. The exam was an invention of the university, not of nature.
105. He was somewhat surprised by how honorable the result felt.

### 第 10 段（16 句）
106. On the last night of the month, the group cleaned the common room.
107. They began to sweep the floor, packed their books, and plugged in the lamp.
108. David wanted to get rid of the old notes, but he kept the summary as a foundation for next term.
109. They stood together and looked out at the campus.
110. The snow had stopped, and the windows were full of light.
111. "What now?" Mark asked.
112. "Now we rest," Sarah said.
113. David felt a quiet triumph, personal and small.
114. The examinations had been the first real test of his new life, and he had not failed.
115. The waiting had been worse than the exams themselves.
116. He was practically a different student.
117. The future was vague, but his viewpoint had changed: the university was no longer a strange place but a home.
118. The term had been a voyage, and he had reached the other side.
119. He did not know where the next year would take him, or what kind of youngster he would become, but he could build a life here, one month at a time.
120. They turned off the lamp, and the night was cold and peaceful.
121. Our story, he thought, had only begun.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
