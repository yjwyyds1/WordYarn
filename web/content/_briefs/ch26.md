## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（8 句）
1. The bus left Haizhou at six, and by the time the rain began, David had stopped counting the turns.
2. He sat near the rear with his bag on the seat beside him and watched the city give way to green fields and then to the low slope of the hills.
3. The conductor, a thin woman with a clipboard, called each stop twice.
4. Nobody else seemed to be in a hurry.
5. A farmer boarded with a net of chickens.
6. A student fell asleep against the window with her mouth open.
7. A fellow passenger offered David a boiled egg, and he took it.
8. He had traveled this road many times, but never with so much on his mind.

### 第 2 段（5 句）
9. The telephone call had come three nights earlier.
10. His grandmother had an infection in her chest, and the village doctor wanted someone from the family beside her.
11. The fever was moderate, he said, and the medicine was working, but at her age a nasty cough could turn worse very quickly.
12. David asked his professor to postpone the last seminar of the term, packed a bag and bought a ticket.
13. There was no purpose in a panic before he had seen her himself.

### 第 3 段（7 句）
14. The cottage stood at the end of the lane, behind a wooden gate that had begun to rust at the hinges.
15. A brief shower had passed, and the light on the wet stones was gorgeous.
16. A hen wandered through a hole in the fence and disappeared into the vegetable patch, where the beans climbed their vertical poles in tidy rows.
17. The well was in the rear yard, its stone base worn smooth by years of buckets, and the water still tasted of iron and rain.
18. His grandmother sat in the comfortable chair by the window with a towel across her knees, her gray hair brushed.
19. "You came all this way," she said.
20. "There was no need." But she held his hand for a long time, and David sensed that she was glad.

### 第 4 段（10 句）
21. The next morning he walked to the clinic on the main street.
22. It occupied two rooms of an old house, with a cupboard of medicines, a sink, and a wooden bench for patients.
23. Doctor Zhou was a retired military doctor who had returned to the village.
24. He showed David a catalogue of medicines and a computer that had never been connected to the internet.
25. "I can treat a cough," he said, "but I cannot format a disk, and I have no idea how to move all this onto a screen." David spent the afternoon transferring patient data into a simple database.
26. The clinic's equipment was old but clean, and its records were consistent: every temperature, every dose, every visit, in the same careful hand.
27. The old catalogue omitted several drugs that the clinic now used, so David made a new list and taught the nurse how to update it.
28. For convenience, he also printed a short guide and taped it inside the cupboard door.
29. The guide proved useful within a week.
30. "You have doubled the capability of this clinic in one afternoon," Doctor Zhou said.

### 第 5 段（9 句）
31. By the third day his grandmother's fever had dropped, and the doctor reduced her medicine.
32. She normally woke before six, but now she slept until eight.
33. David boiled water, cut her food into small pieces, and made sure she took her vitamin with breakfast.
34. The clinic gave him a box of tissue, a bottle of alcohol, and a mask for her bad cough.
35. "Drink plenty of fluid," Doctor Zhou advised, "and do not let her worry." David learned to read the small signs: the way she pushed her plate away, the way her voice lost its energy.
36. She had overcome worse, the doctor said, and he expected a complete recovery.
37. It was his duty to sit with her, though it felt like no duty at all.
38. In the evening she drank the tea he made, and the warmth seemed to refresh her.
39. Color returned slowly to her face.

### 第 6 段（9 句）
40. On Thursday she asked for beef and chicken, and David cooked both badly.
41. She ate them anyway and told him that the creation of a good meal was mostly patience.
42. His mother, a housewife who had never left the village, arrived that evening with a jar of plum jam and a cake whose only decoration was a ring of walnuts.
43. The cake was perfect.
44. A layer of flour covered the table, and the sole of David's shoe left white marks on the floor.
45. David washed the plates at the sink, dried them with a towel, and put them back in the cupboard.
46. His mother sewed a loose button onto his coat with a length of thread.
47. "Your grandmother's attitude is better when you are here," she said.
48. "She does not bother to hide how much she missed you."

### 第 7 段（10 句）
49. The village had changed since his childhood, though not in the ways he expected.
50. A coal lorry now came through twice a week, and its wheels had cracked the edge of the lane.
51. A block of stone had come loose near the well.
52. At the village meeting, held in the small chamber behind the school, the driver's company claimed that the road had never been theirs to repair.
53. The discussion turned into politics before anyone had finished their tea.
54. David had a survey of the damage on his laptop; he stated the facts in four sentences and summarized the rest in a single list.
55. The amount was small, an approximate figure of eight hundred yuan, but the principle mattered.
56. There was an old treaty, signed when the quarry opened, that obliged the company to maintain the lane.
57. David found it in the school archive, between two ledgers, and the company paid.
58. It was not justice, exactly, but the settlement was not unfair.

### 第 8 段（12 句）
59. The school had educated three generations of the village.
60. Its hall still had the old organ, and on the wall a faded photograph showed the drama club performing a play whose script nobody could find.
61. The theme of the play had been the sea.
62. The main activity that week was repairing the hall.
63. David's former physics teacher kept a model rocket, a drawing of a spacecraft, and a photograph of the physicist he had once met in a glass case.
64. On Saturday David helped him mount a new display in the dim light of the hall.
65. His hands were clumsy now, and he spoke about the evolution of the village with the excitement he had once given to a lesson on gravity.
66. "The expansion of the city will reach us in ten years," he said.
67. "You should decide what you want it to look like." His friendship with David had begun over a broken radio when David was twelve, an accidental meeting that had shaped everything after it.
68. David had the impression that the old man had waited all week for someone to say it.
69. "You inspired me," David said, and the old man waved the words away.
70. They could see the whole valley from the roof, a splendid spread of fields and water.

### 第 9 段（13 句）
71. There was no lack of work in the attic.
72. One afternoon David climbed the narrow stairs with a saw and a flashlight, meaning to patch a wooden shelf, and stayed for three hours.
73. A layer of dust covered everything, and the shapes of old furniture looked like animals.
74. He found his grandfather's trunk behind a broken chair.
75. Inside were a uniform, a bundle of letters tied with thread, and a photograph of two young men standing in front of a lorry with their arms around each other.
76. The letters revealed something the family had never spoken of, and gave him his first clue: a quarrel over a gambling debt that had split the village for a decade.
77. The hatred had faded, but the silence had lasted longer than the anger.
78. David felt disgust at the old pride that had kept two families apart.
79. It was a treaty too, he thought, signed by two stubborn men who had finally run out of words.
80. He brought the letters downstairs and gave them to his grandmother.
81. She looked at them for a long time and then said, "Well. It was all a long time ago." Her determination had carried the family through worse than a fever.
82. "You resemble your grandfather," she added.
83. "He never knew how to finish an argument either."

### 第 10 段（9 句）
84. The morning he was to depart, the rain had stopped and the lane smelled of wet stone.
85. His grandmother stood at the gate and watched the bus until it turned the corner.
86. She had pressed a jar of jam into his hands and a present for his mother that she would not let him open.
87. "If you cannot come back in the summer," she said, "write. Anybody can write." David promised that he would bring Mark with him next time, and she said, "Yeah. Bring the boy who eats so much." David was reluctant to leave, but the new term would not wait, and there was nothing else to do.
88. He took his seat near the rear and looked back at the cottage until the road bent and it was gone.
89. He would probably get back to the city before dark.
90. The fields slid past, farther and farther from the village.
91. He thought about the prospect of the coming year and about the quiet determination he had seen in the lane all week.
92. He would take the lesson of those days back: that a place is not only its buildings but the shape of the care people give each other, repeated widely in every village.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
