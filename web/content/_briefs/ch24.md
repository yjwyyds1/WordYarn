## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（9 句）
1. Spring came early that year, mild and bright.
2. Ms. Wu, David's boss at the museum, had a proposal: the museum would launch a children's program for the spring exhibition, and she wanted him to train as a guide.
3. "You have the patience," she said.
4. David was anxious.
5. He had never taught children; he was not sure he could hold their attention.
6. The program was free, funded by the board after months of review; its approval had taken a long time.
7. It would welcome children from six to eleven, and by the first week forty percent of the places were booked.
8. David signed the form, attended two training sessions, and hung a poster in the corridor.
9. The exhibition, "Dragons and Machines," would open at the end of March.

### 第 2 段（6 句）
10. The training tour began in the old galleries.
11. The museum houses four rooms: a rural farm room, with an ax, a barrel, a butter churn, a candle, chalk, a shallow cream bowl, a stool and a wooden chest; a town room, with a lawyer's desk, a jury box, a dentist's chair, an infant's bed and a maid's uniform; a health room, where physical models of a germ and a virus explained disease; and an energy room, with a solar panel, a small motor, a robot and a digital screen.
12. The route allowed wheelchair access.
13. "The structure of the tour is simple," Ms. Wu said.
14. "Four rooms, one story each. Save time by knowing the building; the building is part of the lesson." David learned the emergency exits and the internal door that led to the tunnel.
15. He practiced using the microphone and the visual map, and he learned to adjust the screen's mode with a single touch.

### 第 3 段（13 句）
16. On the first Saturday, twenty children arrived with their parents.
17. The hall was bright and noisy.
18. David felt nervous; his voice shook at the start.
19. "Why do we keep old things?" he asked.
20. A boy said, "Because they are cool." A girl said, "Because they tell us who we were." David smiled and began.
21. In the dragon room, a mechanical dragon with a motor in its chest turned its head and opened its mouth; the children shouted with delight.
22. Its tail moved slowly, and a robot beside it drew circles on a screen.
23. The robot could even spit soap bubbles, and the children ran to catch them.
24. "I bet you cannot draw a perfect circle," David said, and a girl tried.
25. The children could not endure sitting still; they ran from case to case, and they did not tire, though their parents did.
26. A friend from the drama club played a puppet comedy about a dragon who wanted to be a dentist, and a singer taught them a song.
27. One child had a slight flu and wore a mask.
28. It seemed impossible to keep order, but amid the noise, they were learning.

### 第 4 段（13 句）
29. Then a small boy slipped on the ramp by the rail and cut his lip.
30. He cried; his jacket was fine, but the cut was not.
31. David knelt, used his radio to send a colleague for the first-aid kit, and helped loosen the boy's collar.
32. The boy's mother, who had a heart condition, was more frightened than the child.
33. "It's okay," David said.
34. "We will take care of him." There was no time to waste.
35. The staff took her fear into consideration, and the museum's doctor cleaned the cut and did not prescribe anything; the boy was brave.
36. A minute later the fire alarm sounded.
37. Steam from the cafe had triggered the detector, but the staff did not wait to detect the cause.
38. They led everyone through the tunnel to the yard, where a fire officer inspected the exit and the alarm system.
39. David counted the children twice.
40. "A plan of action is worth more than a loud voice," Ms. Wu said.
41. "A false alarm is a cheap lesson; the costly one comes when no one listens." Neither the children nor the parents complained.

### 第 5 段（12 句）
42. On Monday the campus paper's editor called.
43. She wanted a story about the children's program, and the media attention pleased the board.
44. But the exhibition had one controversial label.
45. In the town room, a panel about the war described civilians and soldiers, and a visitor complained that it was too political.
46. Another objected that the section on culture and religion was too small, and a third pointed out that the panel about unemployment in the 1930s said almost nothing about ordinary families.
47. Ms. Wu listened to all of them.
48. "Our objective is not to preach," she said.
49. "It is to show. We can change the label, but we should not change the facts." She asked David for his advice.
50. He said the panel should ask a question instead of giving an answer.
51. "A good exhibit is a conversation," he said.
52. "Not a sermon." Ms. Wu nodded and asked him to help rewrite the label.
53. The editor printed both the complaint and the museum's reply.

### 第 6 段（9 句）
54. The program ended with a drawing competition.
55. The children drew what they had seen: a dragon with a robot arm, a farm with a solar panel, a dentist with a kind face.
56. A girl drew a picture of David holding a microphone and wrote under it, "The best guide." He could feel pure joy.
57. The prize was a small book, and every child received one.
58. The drawings went on display in the gallery, and visitors stopped to look.
59. The program was a great success.
60. David noticed that the children's pictures were more accurate than many adult descriptions.
61. They had understood the equation of old and new, namely that a candle and a solar lamp both give light, and that a horse and a motor both give movement.
62. In the feedback forms, parents used words like "thoughtful" and "wonderful." One wrote, "My son now uses the word 'museum' as a verb." David laughed at the idiom, and then realized how much he would miss the Saturdays.

### 第 7 段（13 句）
63. On the last day, a mother lost her son.
64. She was carrying a suitcase and a hat, and her face went pale.
65. "He was here a minute ago," she said.
66. David stayed calm.
67. He would send two guides to the exit, one to the tunnel, and he himself went back through the rooms.
68. He found the boy in the farm room, hiding inside a large barrel, laughing.
69. "I was playing hide-and-seek," the boy said.
70. David did not shout.
71. He knelt and said, "You scared your mother. Let's go and tell her you are safe." The mother was ashamed of her panic and grateful for the rescue.
72. "Bless you," she said.
73. David said it was nothing.
74. Evidently the boy had slipped away while she was reading a label.
75. That evening David wrote in his notebook: a guide must never retreat from a crying child.

### 第 8 段（11 句）
76. The spring fair filled the yard on the last afternoon.
77. Trees surround the yard, and their leaves were new.
78. The weather was mild, though the sky was cloudy by dusk.
79. Most visitors wore casual clothes.
80. There was a table with pancakes, butter and cream; a small band played; and an old couple sat on a bench and called each other "darling." They had been married for fifty years, and the woman's eyesight was poor, so David read the labels to her.
81. He adjusted his glasses and cleaned a lens with his sleeve.
82. "You are a helpful young man," she said.
83. A child gave him a drawing of a dragon, a gift that was theirs to give and his to keep.
84. Tourists from other cities bought goods in the shop, and the decline in visitors had reversed.
85. Overall, the program had been a success.
86. David felt a quiet pride, and a habit of looking at people, not only objects.

### 第 9 段（8 句）
87. After the fair, David walked through the empty galleries.
88. He realized that the museum had changed him.
89. He had learned to treat a difficult question as a door, not a wall.
90. He had learned to use silence as well as words.
91. In the farm room, he saw a child's chalk drawing on a small board, left behind; he did not erase it.
92. The garden outside was ready for spring: the staff had begun to sow seeds in the flower beds, and a gardener was planting a young tree.
93. The solar lamps along the path came on at dusk, and their reflection in the windows looked like small moons.
94. He thought about the visitors who had come and gone, and about the ones who would come next.

### 第 10 段（13 句）
95. Sarah met him at the gate.
96. She asked whether he would return next semester.
97. "Yes," he said.
98. "I want to keep doing this." She smiled.
99. "You have found a pastime that pays," she said.
100. He laughed.
101. The city was quiet, and the sky had cleared.
102. He thought about the children, the old couple, the mother, the boy in the barrel.
103. A museum, he decided, was not a place where the past was stored; it was a place where people practiced being curious.
104. It had become more than a hobby.
105. He had learned to welcome questions, even the ones he could not answer.
106. That, he thought, was the whole secret.
107. He put his hands in his pockets and walked home with Sarah, past the bright windows of the gallery.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
