## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（9 句）
1. At the end of August, a letter arrived from David's cousin, who taught English at a school in the western mountains.
2. She invited David and two friends for a week, and promised that the train journey alone was worth the fare.
3. Mark was enthusiastic at once; Sarah needed a day to think and then said yes.
4. They packed a bundle of clothes each, checked the timetable, and bought tickets for the last train on Friday.
5. The weather report was good, although the coast was still hot.
6. On the morning of the journey they walked along the beach and past the harbor one last time, then took a bus to the station.
7. They would wake before dawn to catch the train.
8. "I have never been west," Sarah said.
9. "I have," David said, "but not for a decade. I was a child then." He did not admit how much he was looking forward to it.

### 第 2 段（12 句）
10. The train left at seven and would take nine hours.
11. Their carriage was old but comfortable, with leather seats.
12. A notice above the door announced that smoking was forbidden, but an old man in the corner lit a cigarette anyway, and the staff moved him along at the next stop.
13. David bought a packet of biscuits and an apple from the carrier who came through, and Sarah read a book, turning the pages slowly.
14. Mark slept with his mouth open.
15. David checked his phone and deleted an old message.
16. The train climbed through green hills, crossed a river, and began to follow a line of cliffs.
17. An eagle soared above the rock.
18. The view was so exciting that David could not concentrate on his book.
19. He watched the landscape change from fields to pine woods.
20. "Look," Sarah said, pointing.
21. "A castle." On a hill above the valley stood a great gray building with towers and a wall.

### 第 3 段（10 句）
22. The castle was the reason for their first stop.
23. It was a royal fortress, built by a king whose crown and portrait hung in the great hall, and it had once been the center of a small kingdom.
24. Upon the wall hung a map of the old roads.
25. A guide told them about its conquest, its princess, and the precious necklace that had vanished from the treasury four centuries ago.
26. "It was never found," she said.
27. "Some people say it is still in the walls." The story was partly history and partly legend, and David liked it all the more for that.
28. In general, the castle was well preserved.
29. In a glass case they saw a fragment of silk, a page from an old dictionary, and a letter written in a careful translation of the king's speech.
30. A poet had once written about the castle.
31. The guide explained the context of the legend, which was related to the necklace.

### 第 4 段（12 句）
32. From the castle they hiked into the hills.
33. The path was narrow and steep, and the sun was strong, but the air smelled of pine and violet.
34. They walked for seven hours, stopping twice to eat and once to check the map.
35. Sarah led the way; Mark carried the water, and David carried the first-aid kit.
36. By afternoon his legs ached and his shoulders were sore, and Mark had an insect bite on his neck.
37. They reached the top at four and stood looking at the view: horizontal bands of field and forest stretching to the horizon.
38. Sarah loved the outdoors.
39. On the way down Sarah slipped on a stone and cut her knee.
40. David cleaned the wound, put on a bandage, and assured her that it was not deep.
41. "You will live," he said.
42. She laughed, although her face was pale.
43. Mark skipped the last part of the climb and waited at the bottom.

### 第 5 段（12 句）
44. That night they stayed at an inn in a mountain village.
45. The inn stood in a good location, with a view of the valley, a wooden dining room, and a fire.
46. The guest book was full of names from many countries.
47. The owner, an immigrant from the east and the widow of a climber, kept a photograph of her husband above the bar.
48. She was a warm host, and her staff cooked a dinner of soup, bread, and apple pie.
49. The soup had little flesh but good flavor, and the pie was full of calories.
50. "Eat more," she said.
51. "You have a long walk tomorrow." The inn had one double room and two singles, and employed one permanent employee.
52. The village did not manufacture much; it sold wool and cheese.
53. Its water supply came from the stream above the inn.
54. The village was a desirable place to stay, and David said so.
55. The owner's grandmother had been the village's first teacher.

### 第 6 段（13 句）
56. The next day was the village wedding.
57. The whole community gathered in the square, and a band played while the guests danced.
58. A musician played the drum, and the sound made the windows shake.
59. The bride wore a dress of red silk and a silver necklace; the groom, a quiet man in a leather jacket and a red tie, could not stop smiling.
60. His beloved stood beside him, and her dad cried openly.
61. There was a kiss, a long speech, and food.
62. David, who had never seen a village wedding, was absorbed by the warmth of it.
63. It was the biggest event of the year.
64. "They are so merry," Sarah said.
65. "Even the pessimistic ones," Mark said, and everyone laughed.
66. A peculiar custom followed: the guests wrote wishes on paper and burned them.
67. The bride's cousin asked Sarah a hundred questions.
68. The village would elect a new council in the autumn.

### 第 7 段（11 句）
69. On the third day they set out to climb the highest hill in the zone.
70. At the core of the mountain, the guide said, was granite older than the sea.
71. The path was rigid and uneven, and in places they needed both hands.
72. Their guide, an independent woman in her fifties, led them past a cliff split into fragments.
73. "Every stone here was once under water," she said.
74. "The whole mountain is a sample of the old sea floor." David, whose lungs were not used to the altitude, had to stop often to catch his breath.
75. The guide was responsible for their safety, and she watched the sky with a careful eye.
76. She told them about the nitrogen in the soil and the nuclear test proposed years ago for a nearby valley; the plan was stopped by a collective protest.
77. "We are not against science," she said.
78. "We are against carelessness." Her expression was serious.
79. She had quit smoking years before, but still missed it.

### 第 8 段（12 句）
80. Near the top the weather changed.
81. Clouds came up from the west, the light turned gray, and lightning struck the ridge.
82. The force of the storm shook the mountain, and the guide told them to descend at once.
83. They hurried down the path, and David felt fear and terror; his heart beat fast.
84. The guide hid her distress and switched on her lamp.
85. Then Mark slipped and twisted his ankle; he could not walk.
86. The guide tied a stick to his leg and sent David to seek help.
87. At the bottom, a ranger answered his call and told them that he had arrested a man that morning for shooting protected birds.
88. A rescue team came up with a carrier and brought Mark down.
89. The doctor used glue instead of stitches and said the bone was not broken.
90. "You will be sore for a week," he said.
91. Mark recovered slowly.

### 第 9 段（12 句）
92. Mark was disappointed, but the inn had a room available for the night, and the owner's daughter was a nurse.
93. She checked the bandage and promised to look in again in the morning.
94. "Do not be miserable," she said.
95. "You have a good story for your friends." The next day David and Sarah visited the school, where twenty children were learning English.
96. The teacher gave a short dictation, and the children wrote the words in their books.
97. Then she asked David to talk about the city, the sea, and the university.
98. The children listened, and their thinking was quick.
99. One girl asked whether it was true that the city had a metro; another asked whether he had ever seen a famous musician.
100. The village had fame for its silk, and the school fund had bought new books.
101. The children showed them a project about local birds, and the teacher said the school had never had any publicity.
102. When he finished, they clapped.
103. Sarah asked the teacher a question, but she did not reply.

### 第 10 段（14 句）
104. On the last morning they said goodbye to the inn and took the bus to the station.
105. The bus had a breakdown, and they reached the platform with minutes to spare.
106. A tall figure helped them with their bags.
107. The journey home was quiet.
108. Mark's ankle was better, although he still walked slowly, and Sarah had a new necklace made of local silver.
109. David thought about the week.
110. He had gone west expecting a holiday, and found something more.
111. The mountains made a person feel small, but they also made the world seem larger.
112. He had an opportunity now to return, and he intended to take it.
113. The trip had strengthened the bond between the three of them, and they had been good company.
114. It had been, in such a short time, a wise and worthy journey, but too short.
115. "Next year," he said, "we should come for two weeks." "You say that every year," Sarah said.
116. "This time I mean it," he said.
117. The train pulled out, and the mountains slowly vanished behind the clouds.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
