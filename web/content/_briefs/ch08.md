# Winter Home · 寒假回家

## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（12 句）
1. The winter vacation began in the middle of January, and David took the early train home.
2. The term had ended with a small ceremony and a promise to write, and now the city was behind him.
3. He began to wear his warmest coat before dawn.
4. The ride took six hours.
5. He sat by the window and watched the scenery change: the suburbs first, with their rows of new buildings, then the open fields, then the distant hills of home.
6. The sky was clear and the snow was thick on the ground.
7. A train driver's voice came over the speaker, announcing each station.
8. "We are approaching the river," he said.
9. Only a minority of the passengers got off at his station.
10. Above the hills, a bird was riding the wind.
11. He thought about his parents, his grandmother, and the gate of the village.
12. Everything he had missed for a term was waiting somewhere ahead.

### 第 2 段（12 句）
13. His grandmother was standing at the gate when the bus from the station stopped.
14. A red lantern hung above the door, and the village looked smaller than he remembered.
15. The snow made it neater.
16. "You are thinner," she said, and took his bag.
17. In the yard his cousins were throwing a ball of snow at each other, and David joined them.
18. A careless throw hit the window, but nobody was hurt.
19. A fierce wind blew from the fields, and a branch of the old pear tree snapped under its weight.
20. David recognized the smell of wood smoke and plunged into the snow with the children.
21. For ten minutes he was ten years old again.
22. A snowball hit his shoulder.
23. His aunt took a photograph, and the flash lit the yard.
24. "Welcome home," she said.

### 第 3 段（8 句）
25. He entered the house and took off his boots.
26. It was warm inside and smelled of wood smoke.
27. David went into the bathroom to wash his face; the water was heated on the stove, and the kettle was beginning to boil.
28. His mother had cooked pork with grain and vegetables, and the table contained a fresh loaf, a bowl of peaches, and a small plate of sweet cakes.
29. "Eat," his mother said.
30. "You must be weak with hunger." He was, and the food was kind and simple.
31. His room had been cleaned and made neat for his return.
32. He went to lie on the bed for a moment and listened to the house.

### 第 4 段（9 句）
33. His father came in from the yard with the account book.
34. The family's income was modest: his father would grow grain, keep a donkey, and sell vegetables to a merchant in the next town.
35. "The dealer pays a fair price," he said, "but the salary is small, and the profit is small." His father worked with his hands, whereas his uncle worked in an office.
36. "The agricultural sector is changing," he said.
37. "We must calculate with more accuracy." He showed David a chart on the wall, with the monthly earnings in neat columns.
38. Fuel for the stove was expensive, and the price would increase in the spring.
39. There was a tax to pay and insurance for the harvest, and his father had saved for two years and could thus invest a little money in a new machine.
40. "The management of a small farm is not simple," he said.
41. "You must estimate the cost before you spend." David looked at the figures and saw that the wage for a day's work was lower than in the city.

### 第 5 段（10 句）
42. After dinner the family gathered around the television.
43. The news showed an earthquake in a distant province, a building that had collapsed, and an explosion at a factory.
44. "The authorities say nobody was hurt," the announcer said, though a reporter criticized the slow rescue.
45. Then came the historical drama, which his grandmother loved.
46. In that night's episode, a queen plotted the murder of an emperor, an army marched through the snow, and a troop of soldiers searched the palace.
47. The novel it was based on was a long psychological study of power, and the actor who played the emperor was a national celebrity with a strong personality.
48. A map appeared on the screen.
49. "It is nonsense," his father said, "but the costumes are superb." David did not care about the plot, and he did not care about the costumes either.
50. He was happy to sit in the warm room and listen to his family argue about the characters.
51. The congress had passed a new law, the announcer added, and the story returned to the palace.

### 第 6 段（10 句）
52. The next morning they began to prepare for the Spring Festival.
53. The custom in the village was to clean the house from top to bottom, hang new lanterns, and put red paper on every door.
54. David's grandmother wrote the characters with a brush, and her handwriting was steady and beautiful.
55. Her initial letters were a little crooked, but the last ones were perfect.
56. She signed her name at the bottom, and her signature was as steady as her handwriting.
57. "In my day," she said, "every child learned the alphabet before the age of six." David's little cousin was learning English at school, and she practiced a dialog with him: "How are you?" "I am fine, thank you." She could not yet describe her feelings, but she could communicate.
58. She knew the difference between an adjective and a noun.
59. "English is compulsory now," her mother said.
60. "It is good for the future." David had never associated the alphabet with pleasure before, but the child made it seem like a game.
61. Later there was a storytelling session by the fire.

### 第 7 段（10 句）
62. On the eve of the festival the whole family ate together.
63. There was pork and fish, a loaf of bread, a bowl of grain, and a plate of peaches.
64. David was partial to the sweet cakes, and he ate more than was good for him.
65. "Excess is the enemy of health," his grandmother said, but she gave him another cake anyway.
66. At midnight the fireworks began.
67. The first explosion was so loud that the windows shook, and a flash of red light filled the yard.
68. A fountain of sparks rose above the gate and fell back into the snow.
69. The lanterns shone, and the village seemed to shine with them.
70. "May the new year bring you luck," his father said, raising his glass.
71. "And good health." They drank, and the night was no longer gloomy.

### 第 8 段（9 句）
72. On the second day of the festival they went to the temple fair.
73. They entered through a stone gate, and a stone pillar stood at the entrance.
74. The village priest stood at the door of the small temple, and people came to worship and to make wishes for the year.
75. A storyteller told a fairy tale about a queen and a magic wing, and the children listened with open mouths.
76. There were stalls selling straw hats, steel tools, and sweet cakes.
77. A disabled craftsman sold straw birds, and a merchant from the next town sold peaches and grain.
78. A local celebrity, a retired opera singer, sang a song, and everyone snapped photographs.
79. The scenery from the hill above the fair was superb: white roofs, red lanterns, and the curve of the river.
80. David bought a small straw bird for his cousin and a steel hook for his father's workshop.

### 第 9 段（13 句）
81. In the afternoon David went for a walk with his father.
82. They went on foot, because the road was rough and the snow was deep in places.
83. They passed a donkey pulling a cart, a frozen fountain, and an old mill that no longer operated.
84. "It collapsed last year," his father said.
85. "It would cost too much to repair." It was unlikely that the mill would ever reopen.
86. They walked at a slow pace and talked about the future.
87. David's father wanted him to find a good job in the city and earn a salary; David wanted to learn more before he chose.
88. "You cannot hide from the world forever," his father said.
89. "But you can choose your angle." David did not know specifically what he wanted, and it did not occur to him to argue.
90. "How did people cope in the old days?" he asked.
91. "They worked," his father said.
92. "It was nobody's fault, and they did not complain." They saw a bird with an injured wing and tried in vain to catch it.
93. In the distance, two boys were trying to ski on a slope.

### 第 10 段（15 句）
94. On the last evening David sat with his grandmother by the stove.
95. She was old, and her health was not what it had been, but her mind was clear.
96. "When I was your age," she said, "I could not imagine leaving the village. Now I am glad you went." She told him about the years of hunger, and about the earthquake that had shaken the village when she was a girl.
97. "The world is changing," she said.
98. "You must regulate your life, respect your elders, and keep your courage." Every being in the house was asleep except the two of them.
99. David looked at the snow falling above the gate.
100. He did not know what the next term would bring, but he was not afraid.
101. The old nightmare of failing had not returned.
102. The village was ours, and the future was his.
103. Tomorrow he would take the train back to the city.
104. He would learn, practice, and wait.
105. The ultimate test was still ahead, but he was ready, and he would definitely return.
106. He was rather tired, but he did not want the evening to end.
107. "May your year be sweet," his grandmother said.
108. "And yours," he answered.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
