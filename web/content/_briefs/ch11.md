## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（11 句）
1. Summer arrived and the campus emptied.
2. Mark went home to help with the harvest; Sarah left for a museum internship; David stayed.
3. Mark had been absent from the dormitory for a week before that, and the room felt strange without him.
4. Professor Chen, who had been teaching the science workshop, had offered him a place on a small research team, and he had accepted before he understood the work.
5. The pay was small, the laboratory was hot, and the routine began at seven.
6. The bus took ninety minutes and stopped at a crossing near the lab, and David learned to sleep standing up.
7. He had to accustom himself to the silence and to the long commute.
8. By the end of the first week he was waking before the bell of his alarm.
9. He did not know what would happen.
10. He kept a diary in a small notebook, and on the first page he wrote: Do not pretend to understand.
11. Ask.

### 第 2 段（11 句）
12. The project was a survey of the bay.
13. A new sea wall was being built near the old fort, which the council called a progressive project.
14. The fort was a grand stone circle on the headland, built when the coast was the edge of an empire; the museum inside still kept a king's portrait, three rifles, a box of bullets, and a bomb that had never exploded.
15. The fort had once served military intelligence.
16. The guard showed them around and told them about the violence of the old wars.
17. "The fort was never taken," he said.
18. "The sea took more than any army." A spider's web hung in the corner, and a map was pinned to the wall.
19. David admired the guard's calm.
20. A scholar had written a long article about the fort, and the professor gave David the journal.
21. The museum held the copyright of the photographs, used by courtesy of the director.
22. Their assumption was simple: the sea was rising, and the wall would alter the waves.

### 第 3 段（11 句）
23. The equipment was a mixture of the old and the new.
24. The team had a good camera, a laptop, and an up-to-date program for recording the tide, but the sensor that measured salt was cheap and primitive.
25. Its wire was thin, and the connection to the laptop was held together with tape.
26. The laboratory was a converted boathouse, and its lease would expire in September.
27. On the second day the readings began to jump.
28. One minute the water was normal; the next it was as salty as the open sea.
29. David checked the settings, replaced the battery, and cleaned the contacts, but the error remained.
30. "It is invisible," he said.
31. "I cannot see anything wrong." The professor was not worried.
32. "Then we must make it visible," she said.
33. "Expose the wire to the lamp and take it apart."

### 第 4 段（13 句）
34. Life in the lab settled into a pattern.
35. Breakfast was bread and a banana; lunch was a packet of biscuits and a pear; supper was rice and whatever the canteen had left.
36. David kept a glove in his pocket and old trousers for the field.
37. One afternoon a cute fox appeared on the lawn, thin and quick, and stole a biscuit from the table.
38. It would flee when David stood up, but it came back the next day.
39. It was fun to watch.
40. A minor injury to his ankle gave him a reason to sit with ice and a dose of medicine.
41. The street outside the lab was loud until midnight.
42. Mark called from the farm and said the harvest was good, which was a favorable sign.
43. "Are you eating?" he asked.
44. "A banana and a biscuit," David said.
45. "That is not a meal." "It is a necessity," David said.
46. Mark laughed.

### 第 5 段（9 句）
47. The first field trip took them along the highway to a village on the eastern bay.
48. The professor drove an ancient automobile that smelled of salt and old carpet, and the porter helped them arrange and lift the gear into the back.
49. After a long delay, they reached the village at noon.
50. The local council had kept a census since the last century, and an agent showed them the records.
51. An elder, the uncle of a student and an old comrade from the war, invited them in and gave them tea and a lump of sugar.
52. He spoke in his quiet manner about how the tide had changed, and how a young sailor had once tried to swim across the bay and drowned.
53. "The sea is not cruel," he said.
54. "It is merely strong." He said a short prayer, and David recorded the interview in his diary.
55. The village had no entertainment, but that night the moon was spectacular, and the silence was complete.

### 第 6 段（14 句）
56. Back in the lab, the raw data made no sense.
57. The sensor said the water was fresh, while the samples they had collected by hand were salty.
58. One particular reading was impossible.
59. The team began to debate the cause.
60. One student rejected the sensor entirely; another assumed the samples had been contaminated.
61. The problem seemed to stem from the instrument, but nobody could prove it.
62. The first results were hopeless.
63. The professor listened and said nothing.
64. "Do not fall in love with your assumption," she said at last.
65. "Either the instrument is wrong, or our method is." They tested the same water three times and got three different numbers, which was a shock.
66. The outcome was clear: they could not distinguish a real change in the bay from an error in their own equipment.
67. "The numbers are worthless," the student said.
68. "No," the professor said.
69. "They are telling us something. We are just not listening."

### 第 7 段（13 句）
70. David took the sensor apart.
71. Under the tape, the wire was cracked, and salt water had soaked into the copper.
72. The damage was small and almost invisible, but it explained everything: when the tide came in, the water touched the crack and changed the signal.
73. It was a skillful repair: he cleaned the wire, replaced the damaged section, and sealed it with a drop of glue.
74. "Help me hold it," the professor said.
75. The next readings were stable.
76. The error disappeared immediately, and the team's mood changed at once.
77. They could not wait.
78. "You found it," the professor said.
79. "How?" "I stopped trusting the tape," David said.
80. She laughed, which she did rarely.
81. They worked late, and by ten o'clock they had a complete record of the tide.
82. The result was not spectacular — the sea wall would change the shore, as they had expected — but it was solid, and for the first time the project felt like his.

### 第 8 段（12 句）
83. The town council invited the team to present their findings at a public meeting in the old fort.
84. The room was full: fishermen, officials, a journalist, and a group of students.
85. David spoke for twelve minutes.
86. The news excited the village.
87. He used no long words, and he showed one photograph after another, click by click.
88. "The wall will protect the road," he said, "but it will also push the water toward the beach. The wall will transform the beach. It will not disappear, but it will move." There was a short silence, and then the audience began to applaud.
89. Even the fishermen stood.
90. A fisherman offered his opinion: the team had done in one summer what the council had failed to do in fifty years, a terrific achievement.
91. The response was a long round of applause.
92. The professor gave David a quick nod.
93. The team deserved credit, and he felt a shock of pride.
94. They left in a hurry before the questions began.

### 第 9 段（10 句）
95. Afterwards there was supper in the museum courtyard.
96. The council provided bread, cheese, and a large pot of fish soup; someone brought a guitar, and the music drifted over the wall toward the bay.
97. The soup was hot on David's tongue.
98. He sat with the elder's nephew and a young woman, and they talked about the fort, the fishing, and the price of fuel.
99. "You are lucky," the woman said.
100. "You get to leave." "I am not sure that is luck," David said.
101. "It is merely a different life." He could hear the tide coming in, soft and steady.
102. The moon rose over the water.
103. David thought about the long summer, the hot lab, the cracked wire, and the old man who said the sea was merely strong.
104. He had learned more in ten weeks than in a year of lectures, and he knew that he would remember the night for a long time.

### 第 10 段（12 句）
105. The professor asked him to stay for the autumn term and help write the report.
106. The work continued into October.
107. The report was published in a small journal before Christmas, and the team received a grant to continue the survey.
108. David was listed as a co-author, an honor for a student.
109. The success was largely due to the professor, who liked to teach by asking questions rather than giving answers.
110. The project had settled into his mind as a new perspective on science: it was not certainty but a way of asking questions, and it depended on people willing to admit they had been wrong.
111. That was the most valuable thing he had learned.
112. It was no miracle, only patience; indeed, it was the opposite.
113. Soon the campus was full again, and Mark was back from the farm with a bag of pears.
114. "You look different," Mark said.
115. "You look like a scholar." "I came here as a youth," David said, "and now I am content to be a student." Mark laughed.
116. "Nowadays that is the same thing," he said.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
