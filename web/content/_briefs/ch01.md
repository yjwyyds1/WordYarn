## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（8 句）
1. The train from the south pulled into Haizhou at ten past seven on the first Sunday of September.
2. David had spent the night in the narrow cabin of the old carriage, and his legs were stiff, but he did not mind.
3. It was his first day in the city.
4. Through the glass the coastline had opened slowly — a wide gray basin of water, a few fishing boats, and beyond them the low hills that marked the boundary of the city.
5. He had never been anywhere like this.
6. His mother had packed bread, ham and cold onion into a metal box, and he ate it slowly, watching the sea and trying to picture the life he had been dreaming of for two years.
7. He was fortunate, he knew, to be here at all; many of his schoolmates could not afford university.
8. When the train slowed, there was a devil of a wind on the platform, and he stood up, took his bag, and prepared to bid farewell to the sea.

### 第 2 段（10 句）
9. The station was chaos.
10. Porters shouted, families embraced, and a beggar by the exit rattled a paper cup until a policeman moved him along.
11. A sign above the counter warned that it was illegal to beg inside the station, which explained the policeman.
12. The crowd poured toward the taxis, but David, cautious by instinct, followed the sign for the campus bus.
13. The train crew passed him at a run, carrying their cases toward the depot.
14. At a small counter he exchanged some currency, received a paper ticket, and wrote the bus number on the back of his hand.
15. Caution, his father always said, is cheaper than regret.
16. The campus bus did not allow smoking, though the driver allowed the old women to carry their baskets on board.
17. David grabbed a seat by the door and held his bag fast against his knees.
18. He had never been sick on a boat, but the bus was another matter entirely, and he concentrated on the window until the spinning stopped.

### 第 3 段（8 句）
19. The bus was old and slow, which suited him; it gave him time to look.
20. Haizhou University spread along the coast road like a small city of its own.
21. The architecture mixed two centuries and several styles — gray stone halls and glass laboratories — and everywhere there was construction: scaffolds, cranes, and workmen in yellow helmets.
22. A workman on a ladder waved at the bus as it passed.
23. A huge new library was rising behind the old statue of the founder, and its open roof looked like the ribs of some tame animal.
24. The driver, a cheerful man who knew every passenger, played folk songs on the radio and said the library would be finished before the spring festival.
25. It would be the most revolutionary building on the coast, he said: efficient, beautiful, and warm in winter.
26. David listened and did not speak, but he looked at everything, and the city looked back at him.

### 第 4 段（7 句）
27. The dormitory stood near the eastern boundary, a six-story block with a long chimney and a shed for bicycles.
28. David found his room on the fourth floor, opened the door with the key he had been given after paying the deposit, and discovered that his roommate had arrived first.
29. Mark was a broad, eager boy from a farm inland, where his family raised sheep, and he spoke of lamb the way city boys spoke of pizza.
30. He grinned, grabbed David's bag, and introduced himself in one long breath.
31. "You are from the coast, right? I could tell. You smell of salt." The two beds stood parallel under the window, and by evening the room already felt like a small home.
32. Mark, in the easy way of country people, became the brother David had never had.
33. David tried to capture the occasion with his phone's camera, but the photograph came out blurred, and they laughed at it together.

### 第 5 段（9 句）
34. They spent the afternoon unpacking.
35. The room was small and in mild disorder — books on the bed, a kettle on the floor, a picture of Mark's family taped above his desk.
36. Rain confined them indoors on Wednesday, but on Thursday they cooked their first dinner on the shared stove.
37. Mark produced an onion, two eggs and a little rice from his bag, and David, who had never cooked anything more complicated than noodles, watched and learned.
38. "Please, stir this," Mark said, handing him the spoon.
39. The basin of water on the table, the smell of frying onion, the moisture on the window — it was a small, warm scene, and it made the huge city outside seem less strange.
40. They pooled their money for the week, and Mark, of his own accord, carried the garbage down to the bins behind the dorm.
41. "My mother's remedy for everything," he said, "is onion and rice." "My mother's is tea," David said, and Mark laughed so hard he nearly dropped the basin.
42. David's only vice, he confessed, was sweets; his mate Mark's was loud music and the radio's default station, which seemed to play one commercial after another.

### 第 6 段（8 句）
43. Later, lying on his bed with the light off, David found that he could not sleep.
44. He thought about his father, a fisherman who went out before dawn and came back with the tide, and about the dolphin that had once followed their boat for a whole mile.
45. He owed his parents more than he could say, and his father's quiet influence had shaped every choice he had made.
46. His throat felt tight, and he pretended it was the dust.
47. The noise of the city could not escape the closed window; it pressed against the glass like something alive, and the sound raised his suspicion that he would never get used to it.
48. Mark, on the other bed, pretended to be asleep too, and after a pause he spoke into the dark.
49. "It gets better. The first week is a trial." It was an intimate thing to say to somebody he had known for one day, and David took it like medicine.
50. Music, he would learn, was Mark's therapy; that night it was simply the sound of another person breathing.

### 第 7 段（8 句）
51. Monday brought the opening ceremony.
52. The whole faculty gathered in the hall, and volunteers from the student council would give each new student a program, while a student composer played the piano and everyone found their seats.
53. The president spoke about leadership and responsibility, and David's major, engineering, was introduced by the head of the department, a direct woman with an acute sense of humor.
54. She told the new students that half of them would change their minds by Christmas.
55. "That is not a scandal," she said.
56. "That is education." Afterwards they were given their course units, a map of the campus, and a note about the placement exam.
57. The range of choices puzzled David at first; he had never had to assess a course before, or to classify his own interests so carefully.
58. The first period on Tuesday was mathematics, and the professor, who had a low opinion of morning classes, spent it proving that the shortest path between two points was also the laziest.

### 第 8 段（6 句）
59. The first classes were an analysis of everything he thought he knew.
60. The math professor attributed the elegance of his proofs to laziness — "a lazy mathematician looks for the shortest path" — and the physics teacher converted a lecture on movement into a demonstration with a volleyball and the running track outside.
61. In the liberal arts course the students argued about the origin of language, and a girl in a white blouse, whose name was Sarah, made a protest about the reading list that the professor accepted with obvious pleasure.
62. David regarded her with a mixture of admiration and envy, and if he was honest, he was a little jealous of her confidence.
63. He became a member of the volleyball club that week, though he was badly coordinated, and the coach told him his only useful quality was that he never gave up.
64. The exam at the end of the month was his first real experience of university, and he passed it with a mark that surprised nobody more than himself.

### 第 9 段（10 句）
65. By October the little things had arranged themselves into a life, and the situation at home had settled too.
66. David obtained his student card, learned to ride the campus bus without checking the route, and found a quiet seat in the library where the resident advisor said nobody would disturb him.
67. He kept his money in a safe place under his books, and the running track became his remedy for the nights when homesickness came back.
68. The rainy season arrived, and with it the damp that made the walls sweat; the utility bill for the dorm, which they split three ways, was the first money he had ever spent entirely on his own.
69. He had abandoned his first plan to study medicine, and the choice, once made, felt natural.
70. He still missed his homeland, the coast, the wild wind, and the smell of his mother's kitchen, but the city had begun to feel less like a frontier and more like a place he could live.
71. On Sunday evenings he called home, and his father's voice, rough from the sea, made the week complete.
72. "What do you think of it?" his mother asked the first time.
73. "I love it," he said, and was surprised to find that it was true.
74. "Yes," his father said from somewhere behind her, "that is what we hoped." Today, looking back, David cannot decide which was harder: leaving home, or admitting how quickly the new place became one.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
