## 一、逐句译文（按编号顺序，数组元素与编号一一对应）

### 第 1 段（10 句）
1. On the first Monday of July, David took a cab to the office and arrived forty minutes early.
2. The building was a plain eight-story block with a glass hall and a security desk.
3. His department occupied the sixth floor.
4. A young woman gave him a badge and a greeting so warm that he forgot his speech, and showed him to a desk by the window.
5. "Sit anywhere," she said.
6. "The usual place is taken, but the view is better here." The actual work, she warned, was less exciting than the brochures.
7. That was the first honest remark of the day.
8. His first meeting was with Mr. Gu, a veteran engineer of sixty who had spent his career on water projects.
9. "You are curious," Mr. Gu said.
10. "Good. Curiosity is the only basic skill that cannot be taught." The attraction of the job was already clear: people who knew what they were doing.

### 第 2 段（9 句）
11. The firm designed and maintained water systems for the city and the surrounding towns.
12. David's first project was a reservoir in the hills, and he was given the smallest component: a pump house and its engine.
13. The drawings were old, and half the measurements were in millimeters instead of centimeters, so he spent a week checking every width and every joint.
14. "Measure twice," Mr. Gu said.
15. "Then measure again." David learned to assemble a model of the pump on his desk, to modify the design when a pipe would not fit, and to explain the theoretical side to practical people.
16. The project was conventional enough, but its scale was larger than anything at university: the reservoir would supply three towns, and the development of the whole valley depended on it.
17. The government had pushed the schedule forward, and Mr. Gu would lead the team through every stage.
18. Once the drawings were approved, the team could proceed.
19. Beforehand, he made David read the safety file twice.

### 第 3 段（14 句）
20. The first mistake came in his second week.
21. David wrote a sentence in a report that said a pipe had passed inspection when it had only been ordered.
22. The error was small, but it was exactly the kind that could embarrass a whole department.
23. His supervisor, a patient woman named Liu, read the report and said nothing for a long moment.
24. "Did you check this?" she asked.
25. "I suspect you did not." David admitted that he had ignored the second page.
26. "Do not ignore the boring pages," she said.
27. "They are where the mistakes live." He revised the report that afternoon, and Liu read it again.
28. Her only comment was that he should credit the inspector by name.
29. He rolled the old version into a tube and threw it away.
30. The refusal to sign the faulty report had cost the team two days.
31. But Liu was patient, and a careful observer of her young engineers.
32. "Anyone can make one mistake," she said.
33. "The arbitrary ones are the problem." She had a bias toward evidence, not speed.

### 第 4 段（10 句）
34. In August he accompanied Mr. Gu to the reservoir site.
35. They drove for three hours, past fields of cattle and a village that still burned coal in winter.
36. The land around the water was fertile, and the farmers grew vegetables on the slopes.
37. The reservoir itself was deep and quiet, and a small temple stood on the summit of the hill above it, a holy place that the engineers had promised to protect.
38. Mr. Gu showed him the dam and the place where a new pipe would be erected.
39. "Every project has one factor you cannot control," he said.
40. "Here it is the weather." They searched the shoreline for a gap in the fence, and David took samples of the water to test for bacteria and pollution.
41. "If the count is high, the whole plan changes," Mr. Gu said.
42. "The water can affect everything downstream." For protection, the team had planted a line of trees along the bank.
43. In a dry summer the level would dip by a meter.

### 第 5 段（9 句）
44. On Fridays the department ate together at a small restaurant near the office.
45. The food was cheap and the talk was good, and Mr. Gu always raised the first toast.
46. The amusement of the evening came from stories about old projects.
47. Mrs. Han, who worked in accounts, wore a different perfume every Friday and a smart jacket in the latest fashion that her twin daughters had chosen.
48. She had a strong jaw and a straight nose, and when she talked, her whole mouth moved.
49. "One daughter wants to be a doctor," she said, "and the other wants to be an actress." Liu's husband shaved his head, and the office never tired of the joke.
50. Mark, who had a bad cough, came once and spent the evening describing a classic film about a dynasty and its last emperor.
51. David did not dislike these evenings.
52. He was often tempted to stay for a second hour, and he usually did.

### 第 6 段（8 句）
53. The rest of his life arrived by telephone and letter.
54. Sarah was overseas, working for an organization that helped refugees, and her letters were full of languages and nationalities.
55. She had joined a literary club in the capital and was reading a lot of poetry.
56. "My spoken French is still terrible," she wrote, "but I can order coffee with confidence." Mark sent statistics from the railway: how many trains ran late, how much coal the engines used, and how the union was negotiating with the government.
57. "The expense report is the only literature I read now," he wrote.
58. David noticed that Mark had begun to lag in his replies, and he did not blame him.
59. They depended on each other in a new way now, at a distance, and the friendship had to be maintained like a garden.
60. He wrote back every Sunday.

### 第 7 段（8 句）
61. His apartment was small: one room, a kitchen corner, and a spare room he used for storage.
62. The building stood beside a playground.
63. He would keep his childhood in a drawer: a wooden doll his grandmother had carved, a broken toy car, and a photograph of the street where he had learned to ride a bicycle.
64. He put a pot of herbs on the windowsill, and by September the plant was alive and had begun to bloom.
65. When the lease was signed, his mother sent a gift: a candle and a box of wax.
66. He shut the drawer and would sit on the floor, bouncing a tennis ball against the wall until the neighbor knocked.
67. Sometimes he would pick a leaf from the herbs.
68. The stuff of a life, he thought, was mostly small.

### 第 8 段（9 句）
69. In October he had to present the pump design to the client.
70. When he stood up, his voice was thin and his confidence went out of the window.
71. Mr. Gu, in the front row, raised one hand and said, "Slow down. We are not in a hurry." David took a breath and started again.
72. He explained the design, the cost, and the maintenance plan.
73. The client's engineer pressed him on one point, and David solved it with a drawing on the board.
74. "This is effective," the man said at last.
75. "And the potential is clear." On the train home, Mr. Gu told him that he had done well.
76. "You will never master everything," he said.
77. "But you can master the habit of listening." The praise gave David a tremendous amount of confidence, and he felt, for the first time, that his work might have infinite possibilities.

### 第 9 段（11 句）
78. The first real emergency came in November.
79. A storm crossed the coast, and the reservoir rose faster than the forecast had promised.
80. David was at the office when the call came: a pump had failed, and the town below the dam was at risk.
81. The drive up the hill was slow, because the fog was thick and the road was wet.
82. At the site, men were running back and forth with lamps, and someone yelled over the noise of the engine.
83. David's job was to check the pressure and report at every interval of five minutes.
84. For an instant he could not remember the procedure, and then his training returned.
85. The hazard was real, but the team had a plan, and they followed it.
86. By midnight the level had begun to fall.
87. "A lesser constitution would have cracked," Mr. Gu said, and David laughed.
88. On the way home they passed a parade of fire engines.

### 第 10 段（9 句）
89. At the end of December, the department held its yearly dinner.
90. Mr. Gu raised a toast to the new engineers.
91. "Three years ago," he said, "I stopped counting the years. Then this one arrived, and I started again." David looked around the table and recalled his first day, the slow lift and the warm greeting.
92. A lot had changed.
93. He could now read a drawing without guessing, write a report without fear, and speak to a client without his voice shaking.
94. He had not improved as fast as he had hoped, and there were days when the work made him sad, but he no longer thought of quitting.
95. On the way home he called Sarah and Mark, and the three of them talked for an hour.
96. "Next summer," Mark said, "we meet at the old table. One year." David promised he would be there.
97. The first year of work was over, and he was still standing.

## 写作规范（务必照做，全 30 章保持一致）

### 逐句译文
- 一句英文对一句中文，数组顺序 = 下面「逐句译文」里的编号顺序，**不能增删、不能换位**。
- 忠实、自然、文学化的中文；不要逐词硬译，句子要读得通。
- **人名地名一律音译，和第 1 章保持一致**：
  David→大卫，Mark→马克，Sarah→萨拉，Haizhou→海州，Haizhou University→海州大学，
  Lin Yuan（主角本名）→林远，Xiao Han→小韩，其它专有名词同理（拿不准就音译）。
  院校/机构名可保留英文（University of Chicago 等），但**人名必须音译**。
- **中文引号用弯引号 “ ”**（不要用「」或『』），破折号用 ——（两个 U+2014）。
- 数字/时间按中文习惯写：「七点十分」「九月第一个星期日」「四楼」「两个人」。
- 口语对白照原样译成引号里的中文；引号外的大段叙述用正常标点。
- 长句可以在语义转折处断成两句，但**不能改变句子数量**（一句话仍然对应数组里的一个元素）。
- 不要加解释、不要加括号注释、不要在译文里补充原文没有的信息。

### 词汇例句
- 每个词条一行，`.w` 的 `ex` 数组长度 = 该词在词表里的**行数**（一个词性一行）。
  例：`accord` 在词表里占了 2 行（n. 和 v.），那 `ex` 就要 2 个例句，第 1 个对应 n.，第 2 个对应 v.。
- `ipa`：给出音标，格式 `/ˈwɜːd/`，用国际音标，重音用 ˈ 或 ˌ，卷舌用 ː，长音用 ː，美音英音都行但要准确。
- 例句要**贴着该词性那个义项**写，不要一个例子糊弄所有词性。
- 例句是**可迁移的通用句**（不是照抄正文原句），CET4 难度，能体现这个词的典型用法。
- `cn` 是例句的中文翻译，要地道。
- `note` 可选：写用法提示或搭配（如 `abandon oneself to 沉溺于`、`in accord with 与…一致`）。没有特别值得写的就省略这个键。
- 例句里该词本身可以用别的形态（abandoned、abandoning、abandons 都行）。

### 产出格式
只写两个文件，不要改别的：
1. `content/chNN.sentences.json`：`{ "sentences": [ "...", ... ] }`
2. `content/chNN.vocab.json`：`{ "words": { "word": { "ipa": "/.../", "ex": [ { "en": "...", "cn": "...", "note": "..." } ] } } }`

- `words` 的键是**小写单词**；词表里写成 `a/b` 形式的就照抄（如 `exam/examination`、`defense/defence`）。
- 词表里同一个词出现多行时，只在 `words` 里出现**一次**，它的 `ex` 数组按行序放多个例句。
- JSON 用 UTF-8，不要有注释、不要有尾逗号。
- 写完必须自己校验：JSON 能 parse、sentences 数组长度 = 句子数、每个词的 ex 数量 = 它的词表行数。
